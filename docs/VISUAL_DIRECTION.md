# Visual redesign direction

The target is a welcoming social plaza at blue hour, not an empty test arena and not a cyberpunk city.

## Layout

- Central circular dance stage: primary landmark and rhythm activity.
- Fountain: orientation landmark behind or beside the stage.
- Cafe terraces: social/seating activity.
- Shop frontage: future cosmetic and progression hooks.
- Upper promenade: vertical interest and a reason to explore.
- Clear spawn-to-stage route with wide mobile-friendly collision margins.

## Art direction

- Stylized 3D with believable human proportions; no anime treatment.
- Deep blue evening sky with warm amber storefront lighting.
- Turquoise and restrained magenta accents reserved for interactable areas.
- Clean stone, glass, foliage and water; avoid excessive reflective materials on mobile.
- Original assets only, with licence recorded for every third-party model or texture.

## Mobile performance budget

- Prefer modular low-poly architecture, texture atlases, instancing and baked lighting.
- Provide low/medium/high presets.
- Avoid large transparent layers, excessive real-time lights and dense crowds.
- Use NPC pooling and distance-based animation/visibility.
- Preserve a clear HUD and readable silhouettes at phone resolution.
